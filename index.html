Stay With Us Hospedaje

Hill Farm Inn

Hill Farm Inn

Hill Farm is an inn, restaurant and farm just outside of Manchester, VT. We invite you to enjoy the many activities on the property and encourage you to take advantage of all that Manchester and southern Vermont have to offer. We'll share more local recommendations closer to the wedding.

Hill Farm es un hotel, restaurante y granja en las afueras de Manchester, Vermont. Los invitamos a disfrutar de las actividades, la naturaleza y todo lo demás que ofrece el sur de Vermont en el otoño.

We have reserved the entire Inn for our guests. There is a selection of rooms and suites in the Main Inn. The Farm House cottage and the River House can accommodate larger groups. All accommodations include breakfast and complete access to the property.

Hemos reservado todo el hotel para nuestros invitados. El alojamiento en Hill Farm incluye el desayuno y acceso a toda la propiedad.

Travel El Viaje

Travel Information

Información de Viaje

Most guests will find it easiest to rent a car, especially if they want to explore Manchester or the surrounding area during the weekend. For guests who prefer not to drive the full way, train and bus options are also available.

Para la mayoría de los invitados, lo más fácil será alquilar un carro, especialmente si quieren explorar Manchester o los alrededores durante el fin de semana. Para quienes prefieran no manejar todo el trayecto, también hay opciones de tren y bus.

Flying in Vuelo

Flying into Albany

Vuelos a Albany

Best for: Guests flying domestically or connecting through a U.S. hub.

Ideal para: Invitados que viajan dentro de Estados Unidos o hacen conexión por una ciudad estadounidense.

What to book: A flight to Albany International Airport (ALB), then a rental car to Hill Farm — about 1 hour away.

Qué reservar: Un vuelo al Aeropuerto Internacional de Albany (ALB) y luego un carro de alquiler hasta Hill Farm — a aproximadamente 1 hora.

Notes: This is likely the simplest route for many guests. Having a car will also make it easier to explore Manchester during the weekend.

Notas: Probablemente sea la ruta más sencilla para muchos invitados. Tener carro también facilita explorar Manchester durante el fin de semana.

International Internacional

International Routes

Rutas Internacionales

Best for: Guests traveling from Colombia or outside the United States.

Ideal para: Invitados que viajan desde Colombia o desde fuera de Estados Unidos.

What to book: Flights into Boston (BOS) — about 3 hours by car — or New York City (JFK/EWR/LGA) — about 4 hours. From NYC you can also take an Amtrak train to Albany-Rensselaer.

Qué reservar: Vuelos a Boston (BOS) — a aproximadamente 3 horas en carro — o Nueva York (JFK/EWR/LGA) — a unas 4 horas. Desde Nueva York también puedes tomar un tren Amtrak hasta Albany-Rensselaer.

Notes: Compare total travel time carefully — the cheapest flight may not be the easiest route once ground transportation is included.

Notas: Compara bien el tiempo total del viaje — el vuelo más barato no siempre es la ruta más fácil cuando se incluye el transporte terrestre.

No car Sin carro

Train + Bus Option

Opción de Tren y Bus

Best for: Guests who prefer not to drive the full way.

Ideal para: Invitados que prefieren no manejar todo el camino.

What to book: Amtrak to Albany-Rensselaer, then Vermont Translines bus service toward Manchester / Sunderland.

Qué reservar: Tren Amtrak hasta Albany-Rensselaer y luego servicio de bus de Vermont Translines hacia Manchester / Sunderland.

Notes: Please check current schedules before booking. Rural transportation can be limited, especially later in the day.

Notas: Por favor revisa los horarios actuales antes de reservar. El transporte rural puede ser limitado, especialmente más tarde en el día.

FAQ

Preguntas

Can I bring a plus one?

Your plus one will have already been invited.

Should I bring my children?

We would love if you brought your children, but no pressure! We will have multiple babysitters during the wedding reception on Saturday so you can relax and have fun. The Inn and farm are very kid friendly, as is the entire Manchester area. However, if you’d like a weekend away, please use our wedding to take one. Please remember to include your children in your RSVP.

What should I wear?

Código de vestimenta

We encourage everyone to wear what makes them comfortable. That includes sneakers! Friday and Sunday are a great time to wear your jeans or something similar. For Saturday, you should still focus on comfort. There is no need for a suit or cocktail dress unless that is what you want to wear. For women, please dress so you are comfortable with the weather. Please remember that these events will be outside in a field, so pointy heels will not be practical.

Queremos que estén cómodos. El viernes y el domingo son ideales para usar lo que usarían para un almuerzo campestre. Para el sábado, la prioridad debe seguir siendo la comodidad. No es necesario usar traje ni vestido de coctel. Los eventos serán al aire libre, no recomendamos usar tacones.
